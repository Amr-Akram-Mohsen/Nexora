Welcome to Nexora. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Nexora ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information when you use our website, tailored specifically for our audience in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and the broader Middle East region.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Nexora, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Includes your name, email address, and any information provided when you register for an account or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Usage Data: Information about your interactions with our website, such as IP addresses, browser types, saved items, and articles read.
  • Cookies & Tracking: We use cookies to analyze traffic, remember your preferences, and track the performance of outbound affiliate links.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected data to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our platform.
  • Personalize your content recommendations based on your interactions (likes, saves, views).
  • Communicate with you regarding updates, newsletters, and security alerts.
  • Ensure compliance with local laws and regulations in KSA and UAE.

3. Affiliate Link Tracking

Nexora relies on affiliate partnerships. When you click an outbound link to a retailer (such as Amazon SA, Amazon AE, or Noon), a tracking cookie may be placed on your device by the retailer to correctly attribute the sale to us. We do not have access to your personal payment information or shopping cart contents on these external sites.

4. Security & Data Protection

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. While no internet platform is 100% secure, we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@nexora.com.
